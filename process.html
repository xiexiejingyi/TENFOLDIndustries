  1. 01 Ice breaker

    Every project starts with a conversation.

    We get to know your brand, your people and the world you’re trying to create. Bring us a moodboard, a brand book, a reference image or simply an idea.

    We’ll unpack the creative direction, operational needs, budget and timeline together.

  2. 02 Creative direction

    We translate your brand into a distinctive uniform collection.

    From silhouettes and colours to fabrics and functional details, every element is considered to create branded workwear that looks and feels unmistakably yours.

  3. 03 Prototyping

    Good design deserves to be tested in the real world.

    We develop prototypes to study proportion, fit, movement and functionality. Every detail, from fabric weight to pocket placement, buttons and trims, is considered and refined before production begins.

  4. 04 Production

    Once every detail feels right, we bring the collection to life.

    Our production team works closely with our designers to ensure the original creative intent carries through, from the first piece to the last.

  5. 05 Rollout

    From fitting to final delivery, we coordinate every detail of the rollout to ensure a seamless transition from production to your team.

    Alterations and fit adjustments can be arranged where needed.

  6. 06 Beyond delivery

    Uniforms live, evolve and grow with a brand.

    We stay close after launch, supporting reorders, adjustments, new roles and future developments as your business evolves.

    Think of us as your long-term uniform design partner.
