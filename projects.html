[ 02. Spatial Case Studies ]

Uniforms are not corporate merchandise. They are the human architecture of a space.

01

Luxury Hotel · Front of House

Quiet presence, from doorman to housekeeping.

A complete front-of-house collection designed to sit inside the architecture: tonal tailoring drawn from the lobby stone, breathable weaves for long shifts, and silhouettes that read from across a courtyard.

  • ScopeDoormen, reception, concierge, housekeeping
  • Pieces42 across 9 roles
  • MaterialsWool-linen blend, Japanese cotton twill

02

Fine Dining · Chef & Service

Built for the pass, finished for the room.

Chef wear and service uniforms developed as one visual language. Reinforced seams and heat-tolerant cloth in the kitchen; a softened hand and sharper line on the floor, so the brand reads the same from both sides of the pass.

  • ScopeKitchen brigade, sommeliers, floor team, bar
  • Pieces28 across 6 roles
  • MaterialsHeavyweight canvas, washed cotton poplin

03

Corporate Spaces · Team Apparel

A wardrobe, not a dress code.

A modular system of interchangeable pieces that gives teams room to express themselves within a recognisable frame. Fabric drawn from the building's palette, hardware engraved with the mark, everything made to be worn again tomorrow.

  • ScopeClient-facing teams, events, merchandise
  • Pieces16-piece modular wardrobe
  • MaterialsBrushed cotton, recycled wool, cast hardware

The Bespoke Pipeline

From conversation to rollout, and beyond.

Every project moves through six quiet stages. Each one is as refined as the garment it produces.

Start a conversation

  1. 01 — The Conversation

    Every custom uniform project begins with a conversation.

    Share your brand book, moodboard, references or simply an idea. Together, we define the creative direction, operational needs, budget and timeline.

  2. 02 — The Vision

    We translate your brand into a distinctive uniform collection.

    From silhouettes and colours to fabrics and functional details, every element is considered to create branded workwear that looks and feels unmistakably yours.

  3. 03 — The Prototype

    Good uniform design is tested in the real world.

    We develop prototypes to refine fit, movement and function; from fabric weight and pocket placement to buttons, trims and finishing.

  4. 04 — Production

    Once every detail feels right, production begins.

    Our designers and production team work closely together to ensure the approved creative direction is carried through consistently, from the first piece to the last.

  5. 05 — Rollout

    We coordinate fittings, adjustments and final delivery for a smooth rollout across your team.

    Alterations and fit refinements can be arranged where needed.

  6. 06 — Beyond Delivery

    Our partnership continues beyond launch.

    We support reorders, adjustments, new roles and future developments as your brand evolves. Think of us as your long-term uniform design partner.
