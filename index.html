Tenfold Industries

A brand is not only seen in its spaces, products or identity. It is carried by its people.

Tenfold creates custom uniforms and branded apparel that turn brand stories into something tangible, wearable and made for real work. From front-of-house uniforms and chef wear to team merchandise, every collection is developed with equal attention to creativity, functionality and craft.

We consider how every piece fits, moves and performs and how the collection comes together as one distinct visual language.

Because the best uniforms do not simply match. They create presence, strengthen belonging and make a brand instantly recognisable.

This is workwear, reimagined.
