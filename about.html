It’s all about people

Uniforms are more than just clothing. They’re a reflection of identity, a tool for connection, and a source of pride.

At Tenfold, we think with vision, design with imagination, and deliver with quality. From a chef’s jacket that moves with them to a front-of-house uniform that leaves a lasting impression, every detail is designed with purpose. It’s the pride in a server’s smile as they greet a guest, the confidence in a bartender’s step as they craft a drink, and the sense of unity a team feels when they’re dressed to reflect their shared purpose.

For us, it’s a privilege to play a small part in these moments—to create workwear that fosters connection and tells a story.

Welcome to a new era of uniforms.
